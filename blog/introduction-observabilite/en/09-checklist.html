Chapter 9

A practical checklist

Every line comes from something that first went wrong, somewhere in this text or in its sequel.

When instrumenting the service

  • Emit a trace identifier on every request, and put it in every log line. Nothing else on this list matters as much.
  • Format every timestamp with its offset, and set a single time zone everywhere, including the default of ad hoc views.
  • Add exemplars, so that a point on a graph can open a real request.
  • Add the two or three business counters that say what the service is for. They are what makes an incident readable to someone other than its author.

When writing the probes

  • Never put a dependency check in a liveness probe. It may go in the readiness probe, if removing the pod from the Service actually helps.
  • Write the timeout and the failure count of every probe, without relying on the defaults, and give the liveness probe a generous timeout. It exists to catch a process stuck for good, and it must never be able to fail because the process is busy.

When building the dashboards

  • Alert and diagnose on the rate, never on a raw counter compared to a threshold.
  • Look for the measurement that carries the instant of the event when the exact time matters, rather than the position of a sample.
  • Trigger every panel once, on purpose, and confirm that it moves. A panel one has never seen react is decoration.

When setting up alerting

  • Set up alerts and their notifications, phone or e-mail, for critical failures.
  • Fix at the source any alert that stays active permanently, from day one. Silence must be the normal state.

When deploying

  • Keep dashboards and alerting rules in the repository, and never paste a panel from the web interface.
  • Put a deployment annotation on the dashboards.
  • Remember that a green run reports that the desired state was written, and nothing at all about its health.
Ask the running system a question whose answer would be different in case of error. Not "did my command succeed", but "is the world now different from how I wanted it".
